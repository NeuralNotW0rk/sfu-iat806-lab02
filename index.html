Balls Bouncing in 6D (github)

The balls now bounce back and forth in 6 dimensions (x, y, z, r, g, b).
There's some gravity in the y dimension plus drag to bring the balls to a stop.
Click to launch the balls toward the mouse.
Sorry, I don't know where you are clicking in dimensions 3 through 6 so I assume you clicked somewhere close to the center of the hyperscreen.
Number of balls: 'q' to increase, 'a' to decrease
Ball radius: 'w' to increase, 's' to decrease
Press 't' to toggle the secret 7th dimension.
